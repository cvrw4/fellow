# A day with two meetings

One Tuesday for **Aisha Rahman**, a private equity senior associate ([PERSONAS.md](PERSONAS.md)). Her meetings are LP calls, investment committee, diligence, and portfolio reviews. The jobs are the five from [OOUX.md](OOUX.md). The page is the desktop meeting view.

Two meetings, two series. A diligence finding does not become an LP talking point, and a figure the deal team refused to circulate does not travel.

| | Helios diligence | Northbridge LP call |
| --- | --- | --- |
| When | 10:00–11:00 | 14:30–15:15 |
| Who can see it | Deal team only | Deal team only. No bot on the call. |
| Comes from | Yesterday’s management interview | Last quarter’s Northbridge conversation |
| Leaves behind | One finding closed, and one confirmed for tomorrow’s diligence | A commitment to the LP, in a recap the deal team can see |

Yesterday’s management interview, still open when the day starts:

- **Daniel** — confirm the working-capital adjustment. Confirmed.
- **Aisha** — pull what the management team said about margins last quarter. Confirmed, no date yet.
- A suggested line: push the revenue figure to the CRM. The deal team did not accept it. That figure should not travel.

---

## The day

### 8:40 — The day starts

**Walk in ready.** Aisha opens Fellow before either meeting. Helios diligence is at 10:00. The Northbridge LP call is at 14:30. Yesterday’s management interview is still carrying two real commitments and one suggestion. She needs to tell them apart before the diligence session, or the deal team starts from a list she does not trust.

Objects: Meeting, Action item, Person.

### 9:20 — Prep for Helios

**Walk in ready.** The diligence session opens on yesterday’s unfinished work, already written as talking points. Daniel’s adjustment and the margin question are the agenda. The suggested CRM line is still visibly a suggestion. Access reads “deal team only,” as visible as the title. She does not write a second agenda beside the work.

Objects: Meeting, prior Meeting, Action item, Talking point, Person.

### 10:00 — Diligence, live

**Stay in the conversation.** One note. Daniel confirms the working-capital adjustment, and that item closes in the room. The margin question is still open. Aisha confirms she will pull it before tomorrow’s diligence block, with tomorrow’s date, rather than leaving yesterday’s line unchanged.

**Ask without leaving.** She asks what the management team said about margins last quarter. The answer comes from an earlier Helios diligence call, limited to the deal team. She does not reread the transcript.

Objects: Note, Talking point, Action item, Meeting, prior Meeting.

### 11:05 — After diligence

**Leave with owners.** The note holds the finding. Daniel’s item is complete. The margin pull has an owner and tomorrow’s date, so it will be waiting on tomorrow’s Helios session. It stays on the diligence series. The Northbridge call this afternoon does not inherit it, and the refused revenue figure stays refused.

Objects: Note, Action item, Person, Meeting.

### 13:40 — Prep for the LP call

**Walk in ready.** Northbridge opens on last quarter’s conversation: the commitment to send the portfolio update, and the LP’s concern about the add-on timeline. This morning’s Helios findings are not on it.

**Know what is on the record.** Before anyone joins, she can see that the recap stays with the deal team, that capture is botless, and that she can pause. A bot on an LP call is the thing she will not accept.

Objects: Meeting, prior Meeting, Action item, Note, Talking point, Person.

### 14:30 — LP call, live

**Stay in the conversation.** She writes in the note and checks talking points as the call moves. One commitment is confirmed on the call: she will send the portfolio update tomorrow.

**Know what is on the record.** The LP asks to go off the record about a personnel change. She pauses. Participants are notified, and that stretch is absent from the note and the transcript.

Objects: Note, Talking point, Action item, Meeting, Person, Transcript.

### 15:20 — After the LP call

**Leave with owners.** The recap is in the firm’s note format. The portfolio update has an owner and tomorrow’s date, pointed at the Northbridge relationship. The transcript stays one step away, for a sentence she wants to check. What was promised remains if retention later deletes the audio.

Objects: Action item, Person, Note, Transcript.

### 17:30 — The day ends

**Walk in ready**, already true for tomorrow. Tomorrow’s Helios session carries the margin pull, confirmed, deal team only. Tomorrow’s Northbridge follow-up is the portfolio update, on the LP series, not on the diligence agenda. The revenue figure nobody accepted is on neither. Aisha can close the laptop.

Objects: Meeting, Action item, Talking point.

## Where the work sleeps

| When Aisha is done | What is there | From which meeting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Closed today | Daniel’s working-capital adjustment | Helios diligence, 10:00 |
| Tomorrow’s diligence | The margin pull, owner and date confirmed | Helios diligence, 10:00 |
| Tomorrow, LP series | Send the Northbridge portfolio update | Northbridge LP call, 14:30 |
| Kept, audio optional | The finding, and what was promised to the LP | Both |
| Dropped | The revenue figure nobody accepted, and the paused personnel stretch | Both |

The loop from the object model, run twice in one day: open action items become the next meeting’s talking points, and only inside their own series. Diligence stays with the deal team. The LP recap stays with the people entitled to see it.
